## Full description (Play: 4000 max; App Store: same text)

Zuri is a study partner for university students. It turns your lectures, slides and past questions into notes and practice, so you find out what you don't understand before your exam does.

ADD YOUR TIMETABLE IN SECONDS
Take a photo or screenshot of your timetable, or paste it. Zuri reads it and sets up your courses and classes for you to check and save.

RECORD AND UNDERSTAND LECTURES
Tap record in class. Zuri transcribes the lecture and turns it into clear notes and a summary you can come back to.

ASK ABOUT YOUR OWN COURSE
Ask questions and get answers based on your lecture notes and uploaded materials, with the passages they came from.

PRACTISE BEFORE THE EXAM
Make quizzes and flashcards from any material. Add your exam dates and past questions, and Zuri builds a study plan around what you still need to learn.

STAY ON TOP OF YOUR WEEK
Reminders before classes and study sessions, so nothing sneaks up on you.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL
No ads. We never sell your data. Delete your account and everything in it at any time from Settings.

AI can make mistakes. Check important facts against your lecture notes, and use Report on any answer that's wrong.

## Screenshots you need to take
Use the reviewer account with a real timetable added. Don't put made-up data in the app for screenshots. Take them on a real phone:
1. Today screen with classes.
2. Timetable import: the review screen after scanning a timetable.
3. A lecture's notes and summary.
4. Ask: a question with an answer and its sources.
5. A quiz or flashcards.
6. Study plan or exam readiness.

- Play: 2 to 8 phone screenshots (16:9 or 9:16, at least 1080 px), plus a 1024×500 feature graphic.
- App Store: 6.9-inch iPhone screenshots (1320×2868 or 1290×2796), 3 to 10. If you say the app runs on iPad, you also need iPad screenshots. Set iPad support off unless you've tested it (`"supportsTablet": false` is the default here).
